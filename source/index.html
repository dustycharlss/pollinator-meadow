How to play

  

  
🐾 Pollinator Meadow is a cozy puzzle adventure, like The Lost Vikings. Three friends — a bee, a raccoon and a bear — each have one special skill, and no one can finish a level alone. Nobody gets hurt: a wrong move just means trying again.

  

  
🐝 Scout — Honeybee: flies over gaps and deep water, slips through knotholes, rings high bells and lanterns. Too small to push heavy things.

  
🦝 Trickster — Raccoon: pulls levers, climbs vines, digs tunnels, throws acorns at far targets and lays Plank Bridges. Only short hops.

  
🐻 Bruiser — Black Bear: shoves logs and boulders, smashes cracked walls, holds heavy plates, and gives friends a boost up to high ledges.

  
🔁 Swap by tapping a portrait (or 1/2/3). Friends you swap away from stay put — leave one on a pressure plate or lever and it stays held while another goes through. Look for the class badge over each puzzle, then tap 🤝 Interact next to it.

  
🏁 A level is done when all three friends reach the exit. 🌸 Petals are your score, and each level hides one secret ⭐ Starseed.

  
Touch: drag on the left to move · Jump (tap twice for a double jump, hold to flutter) · action button · drag on the right to turn the camera.
Keys: WASD/arrows · Space jump · E action · Q/R camera · Tab / 1–3 swap friend · J journal · Esc pause.

For parents

  

    
What it is
Pollinator Meadow is a three-animal puzzle adventure (Bee the Scout, Raccoon the Trickster, Bear the Bruiser). Kids swap friends, sneak past sleepy critters, and bring lost babies home. A good fit for about ages 8–12; younger kids can play with a grown-up.

    
No ads, no chat, no real-money purchases
The game has no ads, no chat or messaging, and nothing to buy with real money. The Costume Closet uses in-game Petals only.

    
What's saved
Everything stays on this device (browser storage). There is no account. Saved: adventure progress, settings, arcade rounds and best scores, found gear, chosen looks, stars, and the graphics quality level.

    
How to reset
Use Full reset save… at the bottom of Settings. It asks this same quick check, then clears all of the above.

    
How long it takes
Most levels take about 5–8 minutes at a kid's pace. Trailhead Trials and the finales (Festival Glade, Starheart Summit) can run about 10–15 minutes. (Estimates, not stopwatch times.)
