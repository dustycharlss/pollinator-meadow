# Pollinator Meadow

A peaceful 3D platformer (Mario-64 style) set in a diverse meadow. Run, jump, double-jump and glide as a raccoon, robin, opossum or bear — or bumble as a heavy, wobbly bumblebee — or flutter as a honeybee, butterfly, hummingbird, moth or bat — and collect each animal's favourite food (worms and grubs, bugs, honeycomb, berries, nuts, sticks). Food is plentiful and respawns, nothing hunts or hurts anything, and every animal builds up its own home in four stages. Built with Three.js; play it in a phone browser.

**Co-op:** every animal stays where you leave it — tap ⇄ Swap (or Tab / 1–9) to switch, and team up to solve 8 peaceful puzzles for rare special foods that fill your 📖 Field Journal (saved on your device). Flyers have stamina-limited bursts with distinct real-animal flight styles. Music crossfades by season, time of day and puzzle zones.

**Homes & meadow:** every deposit grows your home's flower garden, and each home stage-up (6 / 18 / 40 units) unlocks a species ability (bigger carry, longer reach, faster boost, a bumblebee 'buzz' area gather, cheaper sticks for the opossum…). Completed puzzles bloom the meadow, with fireflies and drifting petals as it flourishes. A glowing ring on the ground around your animal fills as you gather.

**Gear & workbench:** everything you deposit also fills that animal's own wallet. Walk home and tap 🛠️ Gear (or press G) to open the workbench: three silly, visible upgrades per animal — **Range**, **Speed** and **Capacity**, three tiers each, bought with what that animal gathered. A raccoon gets a vacuum cleaner, roller skates and a backpack; the bear a pooper-scooper, sneakers and a picnic basket; the opossum a grabber claw, kick-scooter and a towed wagon; plus goggles and satchels, brushes, jetpacks, headlamps, sonar headsets and more for the others. Gear shows on the animal in play, in the tray, HUD and journal, and really changes pickup reach, speed/stamina and carry size.

**Time, seasons & weather:** a gentle day/night clock runs all the time (animals that sleep through the dark fast-forward through it so nobody gets stuck), and the meadow turns through 🌸 spring, ☀️ summer, 🍂 autumn and ❄️ winter every couple of in-game days — blossoms, golden and red leaves, a snowy white meadow with quieter wildlife. Soft rain, mist, snow and the occasional rainbow drift by (purely cosmetic and calming). Everything is saved, and little baby animals hop and flutter around fully-grown homes and cheer when you drop things off. The 📖 journal now has Foods, Animals and Moments tabs (27 pages) with real animal facts.

Controls: left thumb to move, Jump button (tap twice for a double jump, hold to glide/flutter), Action button for the animal's special move; ⇄ Swap switches animal; 📖 opens the journal; keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space, E, Tab / 0–9, J, G (gear workbench), N (day/night), B (next season), M (menu); the 🌙 and 🌸 buttons do the same by touch.
