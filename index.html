How to play

Fly, run and climb through a peaceful meadow. Tap 🔨 Build to spend what you gather on Flower Beacons, gardens, larders and bridges. Beacons make the meadow around them bloom and slowly spread your territory: nobody loses anything, the wild meadow just grows lusher.

✨ Sparkles mark your creature's favourite food. Carry it home to score points and fill your stockpile. 🌼 Nectar comes from bees, butterflies, hummingbirds, moths and bats; 🪱 forage from raccoons and robins; 🪵 wood from opossums; 🍯 honey from bears. Swap animals to collect them all.

💧 Visit a Beacon that is showing a water drop to tend it: it blooms wider and earns a bonus. 🤝 Solve the meadow's peaceful puzzles for rare foods. Score as much as you can before the clock runs out!

Touch: drag on the left to move · Jump (tap twice for a double jump, hold to flutter) · action button · drag on the right to turn the camera.
Keys: WASD/arrows · Space jump · E action · B build · Q/R camera · Tab / 1–9 switch animal · J journal · Esc pause.
