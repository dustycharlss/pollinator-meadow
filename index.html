🌼 Pollinator Meadow

A peaceful 3D platformer through a living meadow. Run, jump and climb stumps, logs, rocks and bouncy mushrooms. Play a raccoon, robin, opossum or bear — or a bee, butterfly, hummingbird, moth or bat that can flutter for a short while. Everyone is friendly: nobody chases or hurts anybody, and there is plenty of food for all.

✨ Sparkles mark your creature's favourite food: worms, grubs, berries, nuts, sticks, honeycomb or flowers. It all grows back!
👣 Your shadow on the ground shows where you'll land.
🏠 Carry food home to grow your den.
🌟 Find the golden dewdrops at the top of the jump routes.

Touch: drag on the left to move · Jump (tap twice for a double jump, hold to flutter) · action button · drag on the right to turn the camera.
Keys: WASD/arrows · Space jump · E action · Q/R camera · 1–9 creature · N night.
