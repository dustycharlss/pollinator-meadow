# Online play music — credits

Folder: `/workspace/assets-incoming/online/music/`

Original music written and rendered for the N2 online-play cues (lobby loop, friends-joined sting, co-op complete jingle). Soft piano, airy pad, quiet bass, sparse celesta, and (on the short cues) a soft flute — Golden Hour Pastel / palette-B mood, in the same G / Em family and unhurried 72 BPM pocket as the live meadow beds. Stereo Opus `.ogg` (48 kHz) and AAC-LC `.m4a` (44.1 kHz), about −18 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below −3 dBTP. Limiter only (no soft clip).

Every file in this folder, including the MIDI, WAV masters, and `src/compose_online_music.py`, is **CC0 1.0**. No CC-BY, NC, SA, ND, or GPL material is included. Nothing here requires an in-game credit line. No already-mapped project-pollinator track and no OpenGameArt (or other third-party) recording was reused.

No third-party soundfont or sample library was used. Fluidsynth / GM soundfonts were not available in this environment with a verified licence, so the instruments were synthesized from first principles (additive piano with inharmonic partials, chorus pad, decaying celesta partials, sine bass, lightly breathed flute). Rendered audio therefore carries no third-party sample licence.
